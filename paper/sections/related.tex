\textbf{LLM Agents for Analog Topology Synthesis.}
AnalogCoder~\cite{lai2025analogcoder} cast training-free topology synthesis as Python netlist generation with four-stage functional checks across 24 tasks; AnalogAgent~\cite{bao2026analogagent} and MenTeR~\cite{chen2025menter} extend this paradigm with self-evolving playbooks and multi-agent decomposition. Because these evaluations rely on idealized Level-1 MOSFET equations and qualitative functional thresholds (e.g., differential gain exceeding common-mode gain), they leave quantitative multi-metric closure untested. Domain-specialized generators such as Artisan~\cite{chen2024artisan}, AmpAgent~\cite{liu2024ampagent}, and AnalogXpert~\cite{zhang2025analogxpert} focus on operational amplifiers, whereas graph and sequence models including CktGNN~\cite{dong2023cktgnn}, LaMAGIC~\cite{chang2024lamagic,chang2025lamagic2}, AnalogGenie~\cite{gao2025analoggenie}, and FALCON~\cite{mehradfar2025falcon} learn topology distributions offline rather than reasoning from unseen specifications at inference time.

\textbf{Spec-Driven Topology and Sizing.}
AnalogCoder\allowbreak-Pro~\cite{lai2026analogcoderpro} pairs multimodal topology generation with Optuna TPE sizing on BPTM $45\,\text{nm}$ models, optimizing a single scalar figure of merit. AnalogSAGE~\cite{wang2025analogsage} and AaLLM~\cite{habib2026aallm} evaluate multi-metric op-amp synthesis on SKY130, SAFTAC~\cite{huang2026saftac} fine-tunes open weights via simulation-augmented reinforcement learning, and PANDA~\cite{zhang2026panda} and Atelier~\cite{shen2026atelier} incorporate layout-aware stages. As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:compare}, prior end-to-end topology-plus-sizing frameworks verify designs exclusively at the nominal TT corner.

\textbf{Fixed-Topology Sizing and Benchmarks.}
Given a human-designed topology, ADO-LLM~\cite{yin2024adollm}, LEDRO~\cite{kochar2025ledro}, AnaFlow~\cite{ahmadzadeh2025anaflow}, EEsizer~\cite{liu2026eesizer}, AutoSizer~\cite{yu2026autosizer}, and AgenticSizing~\cite{hao2026agenticsizing} combine LLM priors with Bayesian or evolutionary search, complementing RL/BO sizers such as AutoCkt~\cite{settaluri2020autockt}, GCN-RL~\cite{wang2020gcnrl}, GLOVA~\cite{kim2025glova}, and ATLAS~\cite{oh2026atlas} on suites like AnalogGym~\cite{li2024analoggym}. Recent PVT-aware LLM sizers---AnalogScout~\cite{long2026analogscout} (3 OTAs, 9 corners) and OPERA~\cite{saxena2026opera} (6 op-amps, 15 post-layout corners)---as well as commercial-PDK analytical lookup reasoning in CRAFT~\cite{park2026craft} adopt $g_m/I_D$ intuition~\cite{jespers2017gmid} on pre-fixed schematics. Meanwhile, the Long-Horizon Analog Design Bench~\cite{adbench2026longhorizon} demonstrates that general coding agents fail predominantly at simultaneous electrical closure, and AnalogPortBench~\cite{rajabi2026analogportbench} studies cross-node porting. \textsc{SPECTRA} advances beyond prior work by synthesizing \emph{both} circuit topology and an executable, self-predicting $g_m/I_D$ sizing program end-to-end across 12 circuit families and closing full 45-condition PVT specifications under strict call and simulation budgets.
