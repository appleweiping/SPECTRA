\textbf{LLM Agents for Analog Topology Generation.}
AnalogCoder~\cite{lai2025analogcoder} framed training-free topology synthesis as iterative Python netlist generation with four-stage functional checks across 24 tasks; AnalogAgent~\cite{bao2026analogagent} and MenTeR~\cite{chen2025menter} extend this loop through self-improving playbooks and multi-agent task decomposition. Because these evaluations rely on idealized Level-1 square-law MOSFETs and qualitative pass criteria, they leave simultaneous quantitative specification closure untested. Domain-focused generators including Artisan~\cite{chen2024artisan}, AmpAgent~\cite{liu2024ampagent}, and AnalogXpert~\cite{zhang2025analogxpert} target operational amplifiers, whereas learned graph and sequence models such as CktGNN~\cite{dong2023cktgnn}, LaMAGIC~\cite{chang2024lamagic,chang2025lamagic2}, AnalogGenie~\cite{gao2025analoggenie}, and FALCON~\cite{mehradfar2025falcon} train offline on static circuit corpora rather than synthesizing unseen multi-family specifications zero-shot.

\textbf{Specification-Driven End-to-End Synthesis.}
AnalogCoder\allowbreak-Pro~\cite{lai2026analogcoderpro} combines multimodal topology drafting with Optuna TPE sizing on BPTM $45\,\mathrm{nm}$ models to maximize a single scalar figure of merit. AnalogSAGE~\cite{wang2025analogsage} and AaLLM~\cite{habib2026aallm} evaluate multi-metric op-amp synthesis on SKY130, SAFTAC~\cite{huang2026saftac} fine-tunes open language models via simulation-augmented reinforcement learning, and PANDA~\cite{zhang2026panda} and Atelier~\cite{shen2026atelier} integrate layout stages. As summarized in Table~\ref{tab:compare}, prior end-to-end topology-plus-sizing frameworks verify electrical targets solely at the nominal TT corner.

\textbf{Fixed-Topology Sizing and Circuit Benchmarks.}
On human-supplied schematics, ADO-LLM~\cite{yin2024adollm}, LEDRO~\cite{kochar2025ledro}, AnaFlow~\cite{ahmadzadeh2025anaflow}, EEsizer~\cite{liu2026eesizer}, AutoSizer~\cite{yu2026autosizer}, and AgenticSizing~\cite{hao2026agenticsizing} pair LLM guidance with Bayesian or evolutionary optimization alongside RL/BO sizers (AutoCkt~\cite{settaluri2020autockt}, GCN-RL~\cite{wang2020gcnrl}, GLOVA~\cite{kim2025glova}, ATLAS~\cite{oh2026atlas}) on suites such as AnalogGym~\cite{li2024analoggym}. Recent variation-aware sizers, notably AnalogScout~\cite{long2026analogscout} (4 OTAs, 9 corners) and OPERA~\cite{saxena2026opera} (6 op-amps, 15 corners), as well as commercial-node lookup-table reasoning in CRAFT~\cite{park2026craft}, incorporate $g_m/I_D$ intuition~\cite{jespers2017gmid} on pre-fixed topologies. Meanwhile, Long-Horizon Analog Design Bench~\cite{adbench2026longhorizon} and AnalogPortBench~\cite{rajabi2026analogportbench} show that frontier coding agents fail predominantly at simultaneous multi-metric and headroom closure. \textsc{SPECTRA} advances beyond prior work by synthesizing \emph{both} circuit topology and an executable, self-predicting $g_m/I_D$ sizing program across \NPrimaryFamilies{} circuit families with 45-condition PVT closure under strict call and simulation caps.
